# Solana economics and price validation

These are research tools, not educational/practice bets in the product. The app has only the existing devnet game.

Run `node services/keeper/scripts/research.ts replay shadow.jsonl` against the keeper's recorded Solana windows. Candidate-only observations are excluded. The first 70% is a calibration period; reported results use the later 30%. The policies are deterministic random, always UP, always DOWN, momentum, contrarian and late momentum. Directional policies require features recorded before entry cutoff; without them the report marks skipped rows. Supply `matchedShare` and `networkCost` in stake units to include actual matching and transaction cost. Shadow-only input explicitly assumes full matching and zero network cost and cannot support profitability claims. Refunds, coverage and Wilson 95% intervals are included. Ten samples do not establish neutrality or fairness. Overlapping minute outcomes are correlated: the Wilson interval is descriptive, not proof of independent observations; use blocked day/pool bootstrap for a publishable study.

Before claiming a bot-resistant game: retain complete observations and RPC failures, preserve pool selection at decision time, evaluate every selected window (including ties/unpriceable windows), separate pool/date calibration and test sets, test tap-to-confirm latency and failed entries, and reproduce results from immutable input hashes. Features must not include post-cutoff observations. A [real observational baseline](2026-10-10-pumpswap-baseline.md) now covers 3,780 selected windows and includes a chronological held-out period and descriptive pool/day bootstrap. It does not establish a sustained directional edge. Missing pre-cutoff features, device latency and adversarial replay remain unvalidated; raw data are retained locally rather than bundled in Git.

Run `node services/keeper/scripts/research.ts business docs/research/business-scenarios.json`. Three explicitly hypothetical scenarios count fees on the matched bank and subtract RPC, transactions, archive, support, acquisition and net house cost. Every input must use the same currency and period. Replace illustrative figures with receipts and real cohort measurements. Devnet faucet users, operator wallets and shadow rounds are not paying users or revenue. Track acquisition per activated pair, matched/raw stakes, repeat pairs, house contribution, refunds, p95 settlement latency and support time before projecting a business.

For `attack`, supply JSON rows `{name, quoteReserve, relativeMove, swapFee}`. The constant-product spot model reports quote capital and entry fees only. It excludes arbitrage/MEV, unwind losses, holding a 10-second window and pool fees that differ from input. It is not a safe-bank calibration. A real attack replay must perturb both measurement windows, charge all swaps/unwinds, simulate external arbitrage and confirmation latency, and compare profit with **all overlapping pool exposure**. The keeper now subtracts other unfinished rounds' maximum banks from reserve/2500 before scheduling another. This is an operator scheduling policy, not an additional on-chain pool-wide cap. An independent security review and source-diverse economic price validation remain required before mainnet.

Cold start: keep one shared arena; invitations show a common five-minute meetup time and a friend's confirmed bet rather than invented presence. House support remains capped by the existing daily, per-round and wallet rules. Do not promise support all day. Do not raise fees or house limits to hide low matching. Measure matched share, activation/refund rates and real repeat pairs first.
